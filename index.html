Calculator

Work out what an unidentified item is from its shop price. Replicates the game's integer arithmetic exactly — including the rounding tweak, and the artifact and angry-shopkeeper surcharges that land after rounding, which most calculators get wrong.

Intersect two observations to narrow the answer, and rank candidates by how often they actually generate.

Data is generated directly from the NetHack source and the NetHackWiki database dump — object tables and prices from objects.h, Sokoban maps from dat/soko*.lua. The generator scripts are in the repo, so the numbers are reproducible rather than hand-copied.
Game data
Object tables, prices and Sokoban maps are generated from the NetHack source by the DevTeam, under the NetHack General Public License.
Wiki content
Level notes, item terminology and every reference link come from NetHackWiki and its contributors, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.
Sokoban videos
The eight walkthrough videos are the Concise Nethack series by Larry Fluckiger, linked from the NetHackWiki level pages. Embedded with credit; all rights remain with the author.
Design
Styled after SMUI, using the Nord palette by Arctic Ice Studio and JetBrains Mono.
Built with
Mostly generated with Claude Code and Opus 5, reading the NetHack source and NetHackWiki as reference. The game data is parsed out of the C tables by the scripts in the repo rather than typed in by hand, so it can be regenerated and checked.
